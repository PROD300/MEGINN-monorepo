## 1. Yearn Finance

### S — Strengths (сильные стороны Yearn)
- Largest DeFi vault ecosystem ($500M+ TVL)
- Battle-tested smart contracts (multiple audits)
- ERC-4626 V3 with tokenized strategies
- Strong brand и community trust
- Permissionless strategy deployment

### W — Weaknesses (слабые стороны Yearn)
- Complex for non-DeFi users
- No AI features
- No enterprise RBAC or compliance features
- Retail/institutional mix creates UX conflicts
- No direct enterprise sales motion

### O — Opportunities (возможности для OBSIDIAN)
- Enterprise clients seeking AI-powered alternatives
- Institutional DeFi adoption tripling (24% → 75%)
- Gap in AI-native asset management platforms
- Compliance-ready positioning

### T — Threats (угрозы для OBSIDIAN)
- Yearn could add AI features
- Large war chest for development
- Brand recognition advantage
- Could lock up enterprise partnerships first

## 2. Instadapp

### S — Strengths (сильные стороны Instadapp)
- Best multi-chain UX for advanced users
- Flash loan integration
- Modular connector architecture
- $500M+ TVL in smart accounts
- Strong DeFi foundation (Aave, Maker, Compound)

### W — Weaknesses (слабые стороны Instadapp)
- High learning curve for beginners
- No AI assistant
- Complex interface for corporate clients
- No formal RBAC for enterprise teams
- No compliance/GDPR-ready features

### O — Opportunities (возможности для OBSIDIAN)
- Enterprise customers frustrated with complexity
- AI-powered simplification of DeFi
- Team-based access controls
- Audit trail requirements

### T — Threats (угрозы для OBSIDIAN)
- Instadapp could simplify UX
- Could add AI features
- Strong relationships with protocols
- Network effects from existing users

## 3. DeFi Saver

### S — Strengths (сильные стороны DeFi Saver)
- Best-in-class automation (same-block execution)
- 24/7 liquidation protection
- Safety mechanisms with fallbacks
- Strong reputation for reliability
- Transparent fee structure

### W — Weaknesses (слабые стороны DeFi Saver)
- Limited to lending protocols only
- No multi-chain (Ethereum focused)
- No AI — rule-based automation only
- Not suitable for enterprise clients
- No tokenization capabilities

### O — Opportunities (возможности для OBSIDIAN)
- Broader protocol coverage (not just lending)
- AI-enhanced automation
- Multi-chain positioning
- Enterprise-grade features

### T — Threats (угрозы для OBSIDIAN)
- Could expand beyond lending
- Could add multi-chain support
- Strong automation reputation
